Essential AC Prep Kit Tools for Video Production

A professional Assistant Camera (AC) relies on a dedicated prep kit to maintain camera systems, prevent technical downtime, and ensure smooth operation on set. This guide outlines the essential tools required in an AC prep kit for daily maintenance on video productions, detailing the specific instruments needed for optics care, mechanical rigging, marking, and diagnostic troubleshooting.

Optics and Sensor Cleaning

Maintaining spotless glass and sensor surfaces is a primary daily responsibility. Contaminants must be removed safely without damaging anti-reflective coatings.

  • Rocket Air Blower: The first line of defense to remove loose dust and debris from lenses, filters, and sensor cavities without physical contact.
  • Pancro Lens Cleaner: The industry-standard cleaning solution that cuts through grease, fingerprints, and residue without leaving streaks.
  • Kimwipes or Rosco Lens Tissue: Single-use, lint-free optical paper designed to be used once and discarded, eliminating the risk of scratching glass with trapped grit.
  • Anti-Static Brushes: High-grade camel hair or synthetic static-dissipating brushes for dusting matte boxes, filter trays, and lens barrels.
  • Microfiber Cloths: Dedicated solely to wiping camera bodies, monitors, and non-optical surfaces.

Mechanical and Rigging Hand Tools

Camera rigs require frequent rebalancing, accessory adjustments, and bracket securing throughout a production day.

  • Hex/Allen Key Sets: Both imperial and metric folding sets are essential for securing baseplates, dovetails, cage components, and rod mounts.
  • Stubby Flathead Screwdriver: A broad, flathead driver (or a coin-style tool) designed for quick-release tripod plates and 1/4"-20 or 3/8"-16 camera screws.
  • Multi-Tool: A durable pliers-based tool (such as a Leatherman) featuring wire cutters, serrated blades, and utility functions for general set troubleshooting.
  • T-Handle Drivers: Provide extra leverage for hard-to-reach or overtightened screws on shoulder rigs and support arms.
  • Small Adjustable Wrench (Crescent): Useful for tightening monitor mounts, rosette adapters, and specialized rigging nuts.

Measuring and Marking Instruments

Precise measurement ensures accurate focus tracking and consistent shot execution.

  • Soft Measuring Tape: A fiberglass, cloth-style tape measure (at least 25 to 50 feet) with a blunt ring on the end to hook onto the camera’s film plane pin without scratching equipment.
  • Laser Distance Measurer: Used for quick, long-distance split-focus checks when physical tape is impractical.
  • Fine-Point Permanent Markers (Sharpies): Black, metallic silver, and varied colors for labeling media, gear, and user buttons.
  • Dry-Erase and Wet-Erase Pens: For writing on the slate, smart slates, and follow focus marking discs.
  • Chalk: For marking actor floor marks on asphalt, concrete, or carpet where tape cannot stick.

Fasteners and Adhesives

Quick adjustments require secure temporary holding solutions that leave no sticky residue on high-value gear.

  • Camera Tape (Pro Tapes): 1-inch and 2-inch paper tape in various colors for marking, color-coding camera bodies (A-cam, B-cam), and sealing cases.
  • Gaffer Tape: Strong fabric tape for cable dressing, securing power supplies, and general floor management.
  • BongoTies: Elastic bands with wooden toggle closures to neatly manage cable runs along the camera cage and avoid snag hazards.
  • Hook-and-Loop Strips (Velcro): Both adhesive-backed and double-sided strips to secure wireless video transmitters, audio receivers, and focus motors.

Diagnostics and Power Management

Electrical continuity and power reliability must be verified before the camera package moves to the set.

  • Digital Multimeter: Essential for checking battery voltages, verifying D-Tap/P-Tap polarity, and testing cable continuity to protect expensive electronics from reverse polarity damage.
  • Compressed Gas Duster: For clearing dust from heat sinks, cooling fans, and non-optical chassis crevices.
  • Isopropyl Alcohol Wipes (70%+): For cleaning adhesive residue off metal surfaces, slate faces, and accessories.
  • Flashlight or Headlamp: A high-CRI light (preferably with a red-light mode) for working inside dark gear cases or conducting maintenance in low-light environments.
