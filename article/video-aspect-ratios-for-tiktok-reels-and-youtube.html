Video Aspect Ratios for TikTok, Reels, and YouTube

Navigating video aspect ratios across TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube requires a balance between vertical mobile-first formats and traditional widescreen displays. This guide outlines the essential dimensions, platform-specific display quirks, framing techniques, and safe-zone practices needed to produce professional video content that fits seamlessly on every major video platform without losing quality or crucial visual elements.

Essential Dimensions by Platform

Each platform has preferred dimensions that dictate viewer retention and visual clarity:

  • TikTok: The native format is 9:16 (1080 x 1920 pixels). While horizontal videos are technically supported, full-screen vertical orientation drives the highest engagement.
  • Instagram Reels: Standardized at 9:16 (1080 x 1920 pixels). However, Reels also display at 4:5 when viewed in the home feed, and at 1:1 (square) on the profile grid.
  • YouTube Long-Form: The standard format is 16:9 (1920 x 1080 for Full HD or 3840 x 2160 for 4K).
  • YouTube Shorts: Adheres to the 9:16 format (1080 x 1920 pixels), functioning under the same mobile-first design as TikTok and Reels.

Safe Zones and UI Overlays

Each platform covers the screen with interface elements, including captions, engagement icons (likes, comments, shares), profile handles, and sound titles. Placing text or important visual actions under these overlays ruins the viewing experience.

  • Top Margin: Keep critical text and focal points at least 200 to 250 pixels below the top edge to avoid overlapping with status bars and search icons.
  • Bottom Margin: Reserve the bottom 300 to 400 pixels for platform descriptions, automated captions, and audio titles.
  • Right Edge: Leave approximately 100 pixels of clear space on the right side of 9:16 videos to avoid being obscured by the vertical action bar (like, comment, bookmark, and share buttons).
  • The 4:5 Feed Consideration (Reels): Center your primary messaging, text, and faces vertically. When a Reel appears on the main Instagram feed, the top and bottom edges are cropped into a 4:5 window.

Production Techniques for Multi-Platform Delivery

To produce content efficiently for both widescreen and vertical platforms without doubling your shooting schedule, apply these filming and editing workflows:

  • Shoot in Open Gate or High-Resolution 16:9: Filming in 4K at 16:9 provides enough resolution to crop a 1080 x 1920 (9:16) segment from the center without noticeable quality loss. If your camera supports open gate (3:2 or 4:3), use it to capture more vertical information, allowing effortless cropping into both 16:9 and 9:16 in post-production.
  • Center-Weighted Framing: Position the primary subject directly in the center of the frame during production. Use camera monitor grid overlays to ensure the action fits within a 9:16 center box while filming horizontal footage.
  • Reframing Horizontal Content: When adapting 16:9 footage for 9:16 platforms, avoid letterboxing (black bars at the top and bottom). Instead, punch into the footage, utilize split-screen layouts, or apply keyframe tracking to follow the subject across the frame.
